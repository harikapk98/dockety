Privacy policy

Dockety · Version 1.0.0

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Your documents stay on your device

Dockety stores imported originals, text read from documents, review drafts, confirmed records, relationships and reminders in its storage on your iPhone or iPad. Reading and search run on the device. The app does not send your documents or questions to the developer, an AI provider, an advertising service or an analytics service. No online account is created, and the app does not connect to a server operated by the developer.

What Dockety is, and is not

Dockety is a personal and business document workspace for administrative records you choose to import. It is not a password manager. It is not an electronic health record (EHR), medical charting system, or clinical documentation tool, and it must not be used to store patient medical records or protected health information (PHI). Dockety is an independent app and is not affiliated with any bank, insurer, government agency or licensing board.

What the app uses

The app accesses only documents or photographs you select and camera scans you choose to make. It copies imported files; it does not rename or modify your source files. Your selected appearance and device-unlock preference are stored locally.

Device unlock and encryption

Device unlock uses Apple's authentication system (Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode) and is on by default; you can turn it off in Settings. The app does not receive your Face ID data or device passcode. Your saved records and originals are encrypted on the device with a key kept in the iOS Keychain. The key stays on this device, is available only while the device is unlocked, and is not synced or backed up. The app also uses iOS file protection and hides its content when it is not active. Device security still depends on your passcode and operating system.

Retention and deletion

Records and drafts remain on this device until you delete them or remove the app. Delete a document from its detail screen; the confirmation explains which associated information and reminders will be removed. Your original file outside the app is not deleted. Closing the workspace does not erase stored documents. Deleting the app erases its stored records, and the encryption key becomes unusable.

Backup

This release has no cloud sync. Because the encryption key stays on this device, records stored by the app cannot be restored to a different device from a device backup. Keep independent copies of important source documents.

Sharing, tracking and purchases

There are no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics integrations, no sharing features and no in-app purchases. The developer does not receive your document contents through the app. Apple may separately process App Store or diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

Sensitive information

This is not a password manager. Do not import Social Security numbers, passwords, PINs, recovery codes, private keys or full banking or payment-card credentials. Dockety is not an EHR and must not be used for patient medical records or PHI. Detection of sensitive values is not exhaustive. Original documents retain their original contents, even when a summary masks an identifier. Review all extracted suggestions before confirming them.

Policy changes

Changes to data handling will be described in an updated policy. Cloud processing, online accounts or sharing would require updated disclosures before those features are enabled.

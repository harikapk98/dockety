Privacy policy

Dockety · Version 1.1.0

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Your documents stay on your device

Dockety stores imported originals, text read from documents, review drafts, confirmed records, relationships and reminders in its storage on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Reading and search run on the device. The app does not send your documents or questions to the developer, an AI provider, an advertising service or an analytics service. No online account is created, and the app does not connect to a server operated by the developer.

Your records are organized as Personal and one or more businesses. Each business keeps its own documents and records. This organization lives only on your device.

What Dockety is, and is not

Dockety is a personal and business document workspace for administrative records you choose to import. It is not a password manager. It is not an electronic health record (EHR), medical charting system, or clinical documentation tool, and it must not be used to store patient medical records or protected health information (PHI). Dockety is an independent app and is not affiliated with any bank, insurer, government agency or licensing board.

What the app uses

The app accesses only documents or photographs you select and camera scans you choose to make. It copies imported files; it does not rename or modify your source files. Your selected appearance, device-unlock preference, help-tip choices and alert settings are stored locally.

Deadline alerts

When you confirm a document with a renewal, expiry or due date, Dockety can remind you with a notification on this device 60, 30 and 7 days before the date and on the day itself. These notifications are scheduled by iOS on your device; nothing is sent to the developer or to any server. The notification text names only the kind of deadline and how far away it is. It never includes a property address, a person's name, a policy or license number, or an amount. The first time there is something to remind you about, iOS asks for permission. You can decline, and you can turn alerts off, or choose which reminder steps you want, in Settings at any time.

Device unlock and encryption

Device unlock uses Apple's authentication system (Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode) and is on by default; you can turn it off in Settings. The app does not receive your Face ID data or device passcode. Your saved records and originals are encrypted on the device with a key kept in the iOS Keychain. The key is available only while the device is unlocked and is not synced through iCloud Keychain. It is included in encrypted device backups, so your records can be restored onto a new device. The app also uses iOS file protection and hides its content when it is not active. Device security still depends on your passcode and operating system.

Retention and deletion

Records and drafts remain on this device until you delete them or remove the app. You can delete a single document from its detail screen, a record (a property, company or policy) from the record screen, or a whole business with everything in it. Each confirmation explains which stored details and reminders will be removed, and deleting anything also cancels its scheduled alerts. Your original file outside the app is never deleted. Closing the vault does not erase stored documents. Deleting the app erases its stored records, and the encryption key becomes unusable.

Backup

This release has no cloud sync and no Dockety account. Your encrypted records stay on your device. If you back up your device with iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup, the encrypted records and their encryption key are included in that backup and can be restored onto a new device. Dockety does not receive your backup. Keep independent copies of important source documents.

Sharing, tracking and purchases

There are no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics integrations, no sharing features and no in-app purchases. The developer does not receive your document contents through the app. Apple may separately process App Store or diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

Sensitive information

This is not a password manager. Do not import Social Security numbers, passwords, PINs, recovery codes, private keys or full banking or payment-card credentials. Dockety is not an EHR and must not be used for patient medical records or PHI. Detection of sensitive values is not exhaustive. Original documents retain their original contents, even when a summary masks an identifier. Review all extracted suggestions before confirming them.

Policy changes

Changes to data handling will be described in an updated policy. Cloud processing, online accounts or sharing would require updated disclosures before those features are enabled.

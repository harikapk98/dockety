Common questions

Where are my documents stored? Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync in this version.

What is the difference between Personal and a business? Personal holds your home, car, medical and family paperwork. Each business holds its own documents: company letters, licenses, insurance and property tax. When you add a business document, Dockety asks which business it belongs to. Open the Business card on Home to see each business and everything filed under it.

How do deadline alerts work? After you confirm a document with a renewal, expiry or due date, Dockety reminds you 60, 30 and 7 days before and on the day, at 9 AM. The alert names only the kind of deadline, never the property, person or number. Turn alerts on or off, or choose the steps you want, in More, then Settings. iOS asks for permission the first time there is something to remind you about.

I changed phones. Can I move my records? Yes, if you restore the new phone from an iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup. The encrypted records and their key are included. If you set up the new phone without a backup, your records are not carried over; keep your original files and add them again.

How do I delete something? Open a document and tap Delete document. Open a record (a property, company or policy) and tap Delete this record. To remove a whole business, open it from the Business card on Home and tap Edit or delete this business. Personal cannot be deleted. Your original files outside Dockety are never touched.

Face ID or passcode is not working. Dockety uses your device unlock. If your phone has no passcode, the app opens without a lock. You can turn the lock off in Settings.

Is Dockety a password manager or a medical records app? No. Please keep passwords, Social Security numbers and patient records out of the app.
